Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Most features take just a few minutes each. Even the full menu is usually done in well under an hour, including the backup and testing.

Can my car have every feature?

Every feature needs iDrive 7 and a build date before March 2021. A few also need specific equipment:

  • Full digital cluster (Live Cockpit Professional): cluster style, start-up badge, Assisted Driving View.
  • Driving Assistant Professional: Assisted Driving View, Speed Limit Assist auto-adjust, Emergency Stop Assistant.
  • Factory heated seats: automatic heated seats. We can't add heating to seats that don't have it.

Auto start/stop coding (memory or always off) also depends on your car's software: it may not work after BMW's late-2023 update. Use the checker above to see which features fit your car, and we'll confirm from your VIN before you book.

How do the deals work?

The 9 quick features come in packs: any 3 for $39, any 5 for $65, or all 9 for $99. A pack covers your most expensive picks, and anything extra is at its normal price. Automatic heated seats, Assisted Driving View, Speed Limit Assist and Emergency Stop Assistant are bigger jobs, so they're always full price and not part of these deals. Heated seats does drop from $79 to $39 when you book it with remote start. Quick extras are $5 each and aren't part of the deals either. The page works out the best price for you automatically.

Can I add these to a remote start booking?

Yes. Tick them together on the quote page and we'll do everything in the same visit. Automatic heated seats is just $39 (normally $79) when booked with remote start.

How does automatic heated seats work?

You set a temperature and heat level in iDrive, separately for driver and passenger. When it's colder than your setting, the seats switch on by themselves once the driver's seatbelt is fastened. It's for 1–4 Series and X1–X4 models; higher models like the 5 Series usually have it already.

What if a feature doesn't work on my car?

You don't pay for it. We assess your car first, and any feature that won't code, or that your car already has, comes off the bill. You only pay for what's working.

What is Emergency Stop Assistant?

It's an emergency aid for when the driver becomes unwell. Pull and hold the parking brake switch while driving, and the car slows down in the breakdown lane if possible (otherwise in its own lane), stops, switches on the hazard lights and starts an emergency call, which you can cancel. It needs Driving Assistant Professional.

BMW doesn't offer it in Australia, so please read this before booking: it's there for emergencies only, the driver remains responsible for the car at all times, and we recommend letting your insurer know it's been activated.

Is Speed Limit Assist safe to rely on?

It's a driver aid. It adjusts your cruise control to the speed limits the car reads, but sign reading isn't always right, so you're still responsible for your speed.

Can a badge or cluster style be removed later?

Yes. Style changes are purely cosmetic, and we can switch them back any time, for example before you sell the car so a buyer isn't misled.

Can it be undone? Is it safe?

Yes, it can be undone. We save a full backup of your car's factory setup before changing anything, so we can restore it at any time. If you're concerned about your new-car warranty, check with your dealer first.

Will a BMW software update remove it?

It can. A dealer software update or a BMW over-the-air update can reset the coding. If that happens, get in touch and we'll re-code it.
