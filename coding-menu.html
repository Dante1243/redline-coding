The menu

We'll confirm which features your car can have from its VIN before we book anything.

Comfort

Your heated seats switch on by themselves when it's colder than the temperature you choose, even part-way through a drive, at the heat level you choose. Change both any time in iDrive, separately for driver and passenger.

For 1–4 Series and X1–X4. Higher models like the 5 Series usually have it from the factory. It switches on once the driver's seatbelt is fastened.

Your choice: the car remembers when you switch auto start/stop off, or auto start/stop starts switched off every drive. Either way, no more pressing the button each time you get in.

May not work on cars that have had BMW's late-2023 software update (11/2023) or newer. We check your car's software version first. Tell us in your quote which option you want.

Adds a 5-flash option to your iDrive lighting settings, so one tap of the indicator stalk can flash 5 times instead of 3. Handy for freeway lane changes, and you can switch back to 3 any time.

Removes the warning pop-ups you have to click through: the legal disclaimer on iDrive at start-up, the warning on the reversing camera screen, and the Night Vision warning if your car has it.

A dealer software update can bring the warnings back. We can remove them again.

Driving

Adds Sport Plus and Comfort Plus to your drive mode button, for a sharper or softer drive than the standard modes.

Changes throttle, steering and gearbox response. The ride only gets firmer or softer if your car has adaptive suspension.

Choose the driving mode your car starts in every time, such as Sport, Sport Individual, Comfort Plus or Eco Pro, so you don't have to press the mode button on every drive.

Tell us which mode you want when you book.

Shows your car, the lanes and the cars around you live on the instrument cluster while the driver assistance is on.

Active Cruise Control adjusts the gap to the car ahead by itself to suit the situation, instead of holding the one fixed distance you set on the wheel. It closes up in stop-start traffic so cars are less likely to cut in, and leaves more room in the wet. Switch it on or off any time in iDrive.

Shows up in iDrive under Speed Assistant as "Adjust distance acc. to situation".

Safety

A short chime whenever the speed limit changes, so you notice a new limit straight away without looking down at the cluster. Handy for school zones. Switch it on or off any time in iDrive.

Shows up in iDrive under Speed Assistant as "Warning when changes".

Under hard braking, your brake lights flash to warn the driver behind that you're stopping fast.

Works with the factory Australian-spec tail lights.

Style

Change the instrument cluster's look: the M340i layout with a 330 km/h speedo, the M3 / M4 layout with shift lights, the M-car or M Sport layouts, or the Alpina style.

Choose from over 80 badges, from M, M Sport and Alpina to M3 CS, M4 CSL, M5 Competition, X5 M Competition and V12, to show when you start the car.

Purely cosmetic, and we can remove it any time, e.g. before you sell the car.

Change the animation on the iDrive screen when you start the car: BMW M, Alpina, BMW i, or even Rolls-Royce.

Which animations are available depends on your car's iDrive software version.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take?

Most features take just a few minutes each. Even the full menu is usually done in well under an hour, including the backup and testing.

Can my car have every feature?

Every feature needs iDrive 7 and a build date before March 2021. A few also need specific equipment:

  • Full digital cluster (Live Cockpit Professional): cluster style, start-up badge, Assisted Driving View.
  • Driving Assistant Professional: Assisted Driving View.
  • Factory heated seats: automatic heated seats. We can't add heating to seats that don't have it.

Auto start/stop coding (memory or always off) also depends on your car's software: it may not work after BMW's late-2023 update. Use the checker above to see which features fit your car, and we'll confirm from your VIN before you book.

How do the deals work?

The 11 quick features come in packs: any 3 for $39, any 5 for $65, or all 11 for $99. Anything on top of a pack is at its normal price. Automatic heated seats and Assisted Driving View are bigger jobs, so they're always full price and not part of these deals. Heated seats drops from $79 to $39 when you book it with remote start. Quick extras are $5 each and aren't part of the deals either.

Can I add these to a remote start booking?

Yes, and we'll do everything in the same visit. Automatic heated seats is just $39 (normally $79) when booked with remote start.

How does automatic heated seats work?

You set a temperature and heat level in iDrive, separately for driver and passenger. When it's colder than your setting, the seats switch on by themselves once the driver's seatbelt is fastened. We set it to keep checking while you drive, so they still come on if you leave a warm garage into a cold morning. It's for 1–4 Series and X1–X4 models; higher models like the 5 Series usually have it already.

What if a feature doesn't work on my car?

You don't pay for it. We assess your car first, and any feature that won't code, or that your car already has, comes off the bill. You only pay for what's working.

Can a badge or cluster style be removed later?

Yes. Style changes are purely cosmetic, and we can switch them back any time, for example before you sell the car.

Can it be undone? Is it safe?

Yes, it can be undone. We save a full backup of your car's factory setup before changing anything, so we can restore it at any time. If you're concerned about your new-car warranty, check with your dealer first.

Will a BMW software update remove it?

It can. A dealer software update or a BMW over-the-air update can reset the coding. If that happens, get in touch and we'll re-code it, free within 3 months of your job.
