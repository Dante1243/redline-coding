Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Higher-spec BMWs like the 5 Series can switch the seat heating on automatically when it's cold. Many lower models have the same heated seats but not the automatic mode. We connect a laptop to the car's diagnostic (OBD) port, back up the factory setup and code the automatic mode in. No hardware is added.

Which cars are eligible?

BMW 1 Series (F40), 2 Series Gran Coupe (F44), 3 Series (G20/G21), 4 Series Coupe and Convertible (G22/G23) and X1–X4. The car must:

  • Already have factory heated seats. You'll see a button with a seat and wavy lines.
  • Be built before March 2021. From March 2021, BMW locked coding on its cars.

Some X1 and X2 models use an older system, so we confirm those from the VIN. Use the checker or send us your VIN to be sure.

My car doesn't have heated seats. Can you add them?

Not by coding. Heated seats need heating elements inside the seat, so this service only works on cars that already have them.

Can I change the temperature and heat level later?

Yes. We set it up to your preference, and you can change the temperature and heat level yourself any time in iDrive.

How long does it take?

About 15 minutes, including the backup and testing. If you add it to a remote start booking, we do both in the same visit, and heated seats are just $39.

How much does it cost?

From $79 AUD, or just $39 when added to a remote start booking (bundle from $238, saving $40). We confirm your exact price after checking your VIN, before we book anything in.

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

Either. We're mobile across Perth and can do it at your home or workplace, or you can drop the car off with us.

What if my car can't be coded?

You don't pay. If the car won't accept the coding, there's no charge. You only pay once it's working.

Can it be undone? Is it safe?

Yes, it can be undone. We save a full backup of your car's factory setup before changing anything, so we can restore it at any time. If you're concerned about your new-car warranty, check with your dealer first.

Will a BMW software update remove it?

It can. A dealer software update or a BMW over-the-air update can reset the coding. If that happens, get in touch and we'll re-code it.
