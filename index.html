Frequently asked questions

What is coding?

Your BMW's modules have features in their software that are switched off unless the car was ordered with them. Coding changes those settings to turn the features on. We connect a laptop to the car's diagnostic (OBD) port. Nothing is added or rewired.

How long does it take?

It depends on the feature. Automatic heated seats take about 15 minutes, and remote engine start about an hour. If you book several features, we do them all in the same visit.

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

Either. We're mobile across Perth and can do it at your home or workplace. You can also drop the car off with us. Let us know which you'd prefer when you enquire.

What do I need to provide?

Just the car and its key. For a mobile visit, please park near a standard power point, because we run a battery charger and laptop so the car's voltage stays steady while we code. Please also unplug anything connected to the OBD port under the dashboard, such as tuning dongles, trackers or insurance devices.

Which cars can you code?

Mainly BMWs built before March 2021 with iDrive 7. From March 2021, BMW locked coding on its cars. Each service has its own list of eligible models and a checker. Pick a service above, or send us your VIN and we'll tell you what your car can have.

What if my car can't be coded?

You don't pay. We check eligibility first, and if the car won't accept the coding, there's no charge. You only pay once it's working.

Can it be undone? Is it safe?

Yes, it can be undone. We save a full backup of your car's factory setup before changing anything, so we can restore it at any time. The coding only turns on features already in your car's software. If you're concerned about your new-car warranty, check with your dealer first.

Will a BMW software update remove it?

It can. A dealer software update or a BMW over-the-air update can reset the coding. If that happens, get in touch and we'll re-code it.

Can you do a feature that isn't listed?

Possibly. Send an enquiry with "Something else" ticked, tell us what you're after and include your VIN. We'll let you know if your car can have it.
