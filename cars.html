Cars we can't code

BMW locked coding on cars built from March 2021, and on newer models with iDrive 8 or later.

BMW 4 Series

  • G26 Gran Coupe (4-door)The 4 Series Gran Coupe went into production in late 2021, after BMW locked coding, so no build date qualifies.

BMW 5 Series

  • G30 / G31 pre-facelift (2017 – mid-2020) No remote start, Coding Menu OKPre-facelift 5 Series cars don't have the iDrive 7 head unit that this feature needs.
  • G60 / G61 (2023+)Current-generation cars are coding-locked from launch (iDrive 8.5+ and NCD 2.0/3.0).

BMW 7 Series

  • G70 (2022+)Current-generation cars are coding-locked from launch (iDrive 8.5+ and NCD 2.0/3.0).

BMW M2

  • G87 (2023+)The G87 M2 is coding-locked from launch (iDrive 8 and NCD 2.0).

BMW M3

  • G80The G80 M3 is coding-locked from launch, so no build date qualifies.

BMW M4

  • G82 Coupe
  • G83 Convertible

The G82/G83 M4 is coding-locked from launch, so no build date qualifies.

BMW M5

  • G90 (2024+)Current-generation cars are coding-locked from launch (iDrive 8.5+ and NCD 2.0/3.0).

BMW i4 / iX / other electric

  • Any fully electric BMWElectric cars have no engine control unit to code remote start into.
